1. **First-run isolation timing (stories 83-84; Fork identity).** Final identity inputs are described as pre-publication, but an ordinary development run can already touch upstream-owned state. Changing only Tauri's ID leaves shared control/shell paths. Proposed: require complete application/storage/control/shell/hook namespace isolation before any nonisolated run; isolated baseline profiles remain allowed while branding is deferred. Blocks ordinary fork execution and identity-dependent tickets, not read-only work or isolated experiments. Evidence: [application ID](https://github.com/kevsmir02/terax-ai/blob/cb48a3449a566d66725e2daecc4a21276e75b0ff/src-tauri/tauri.conf.json#L5), [control descriptor](https://github.com/kevsmir02/terax-ai/blob/cb48a3449a566d66725e2daecc4a21276e75b0ff/src-tauri/src/modules/control.rs#L755), [shell paths](https://github.com/kevsmir02/terax-ai/blob/cb48a3449a566d66725e2daecc4a21276e75b0ff/src-tauri/src/modules/pty/shell_init.rs#L447). Test: upstream sentinel files/configuration/descriptors remain unchanged in coexistence tests.

2. **Concurrent app-owned saves (stories 29, 43-45; Editing and conflicts).** Two Spaces can have distinct buffers for one path, both check the same mtime and both report successful unconditional writes. Proposed: coordinate app-owned writes by canonical file identity; compare an expected disk revision inside that coordination, distinguish same-mtime changed contents, and bind explicit overwrite consent to the reviewed disk revision. Missing/unreadable/replaced targets require conflict/error handling, not unconditional recreation. Blocks save/conflict tickets. Evidence: [Space-local deduplication](https://github.com/kevsmir02/terax-ai/blob/cb48a3449a566d66725e2daecc4a21276e75b0ff/src/modules/tabs/lib/useTabs.ts#L205), [frontend save check](https://github.com/kevsmir02/terax-ai/blob/cb48a3449a566d66725e2daecc4a21276e75b0ff/src/modules/editor/lib/useDocument.ts#L78), [native write](https://github.com/kevsmir02/terax-ai/blob/cb48a3449a566d66725e2daecc4a21276e75b0ff/src-tauri/src/modules/fs/file.rs#L163). Test: one conflicting save succeeds, the other refuses and retains its buffer; same-mtime content changes are detected. The accepted external-writer race caveat remains.

3. **Recovery identity and stale completion (stories 46-51; Recovery drafts).** A save of revision A can complete after the user has typed revision B; path-only draft cleanup could delete B or another Space's draft. Proposed: stable buffer identity plus original-file identity and revision-aware persistence/cleanup; older save/discard/write completions cannot remove or resurrect newer/unrelated drafts. A save-and-close cannot close over newer unsaved edits. Blocks recovery persistence/cleanup and close integration. Evidence: [save intentionally leaves later edits dirty](https://github.com/kevsmir02/terax-ai/blob/cb48a3449a566d66725e2daecc4a21276e75b0ff/src/modules/editor/lib/useDocument.ts#L61). Test: delayed save/persistence, newer typing, two Spaces and restart preserve the last acknowledged unresolved revisions separately.

4. **Durable recovery promise (stories 34-36, 46-51).** No recovery write cadence, acknowledgement boundary or failed-quit-flush rule is defined. Idle-only debounce could postpone persistence forever; inherited close behavior permits a repeat close after a failed flush. Proposed: maximum two-second write-scheduling interval during continuous edits while healthy; only successfully durable revisions are acknowledged, pending/failure is visible, and quit uses a bounded flush with retry/cancel/explicit close-with-loss. A repeated close alone is not consent. Actual I/O stalls may delay acknowledgement, and crashes can lose text newer than the last acknowledgement. Blocks recovery/shutdown acceptance, not unrelated features. Evidence: [current close flush behavior belongs to AI history](https://github.com/kevsmir02/terax-ai/blob/cb48a3449a566d66725e2daecc4a21276e75b0ff/src/app/hooks/useAppCloseGuard.ts#L28). Test: continuous edits, crash after acknowledgement, failed storage, pending flush and repeated close events.

5. **Destructive guards across Spaces (stories 29, 32, 54, 59, 64).** A restore/removal initiated in one Space can miss dirty editors or running agents in another Space using the same worktree, and asynchronous confirmation can go stale. Proposed: guard canonical affected worktree/file usage across all Spaces/panes, recheck just before mutation, and refuse or prevent new conflicting app-owned saves/spawns/mutations during the operation. Force never silently bypasses unsaved-buffer protection. External writers remain outside perfect detection. Blocks destructive Git/worktree/lifecycle tickets. Evidence: [selected-tab close guard](https://github.com/kevsmir02/terax-ai/blob/cb48a3449a566d66725e2daecc4a21276e75b0ff/src/app/hooks/useTabCloseGuards.ts#L63), [restore command](https://github.com/kevsmir02/terax-ai/blob/cb48a3449a566d66725e2daecc4a21276e75b0ff/src-tauri/src/modules/git/commands_review.rs#L188). Test: two Spaces sharing a checkout, outside-root editor, and work starting after confirmation produce refusal without file/index mutation.

6. **Worktree removal versus checkpoint retention (stories 54, 57-62, 64).** Removing a linked worktree deletes the identity/admin state needed to list or restore its checkpoints, leaving shared refs retained but inaccessible through normal app contracts. Proposed version-one rule: refuse removal while that worktree's checkpoints or safety refs remain; require their explicit deletion first. Archival restoration after deletion remains deferred rather than added implicitly. Blocks worktree-removal acceptance. Evidence: [checkpoint owner identity](https://github.com/kevsmir02/terax-ai/blob/cb48a3449a566d66725e2daecc4a21276e75b0ff/src-tauri/src/modules/git/checkpoint.rs#L59), [checkpoint list](https://github.com/kevsmir02/terax-ai/blob/cb48a3449a566d66725e2daecc4a21276e75b0ff/src-tauri/src/modules/git/checkpoint.rs#L831), [worktree removal](https://github.com/kevsmir02/terax-ai/blob/cb48a3449a566d66725e2daecc4a21276e75b0ff/src-tauri/src/modules/git/worktree.rs#L1281). Test: either ref type blocks removal, explicit cleanup is scoped, and unrelated recovery refs remain untouched.

7. **Safety-capture failure before restore (stories 59-62).** Wording about partial failures could be interpreted to allow overwriting before required safety capture has succeeded. Proposed: preserve the existing fail-before-mutation invariant; failed, incomplete-for-affected-content or unpublished required safety capture refuses without changing files/index, and force cannot bypass it. Failures after mutation retain the safety ref, report partial effects and require a fresh preview before retry. Blocks checkpoint-restore acceptance. Evidence: [safety preparation precedes mutation](https://github.com/kevsmir02/terax-ai/blob/cb48a3449a566d66725e2daecc4a21276e75b0ff/src-tauri/src/modules/git/checkpoint.rs#L1253). Test: snapshot/ref/limit failure leaves files/index unchanged; post-mutation failure retains recovery information.

8. **Updater safe interval and handoff (stories 88-93).** Work can start while download or privilege prompts are pending after an initial idle check. Proposed: recheck immediately before starting an app-owned transaction; cancel/refuse if no longer safe and prevent new conflicting app work throughout transaction/privilege waiting. If that cannot be enforced, hand off after safe closure under the accepted fallback. Distinguish handed-off from installed; external package-manager actions are not app-enforced. Blocks in-app installer tickets, not update discovery. Evidence: [asynchronous current update path](https://github.com/kevsmir02/terax-ai/blob/cb48a3449a566d66725e2daecc4a21276e75b0ff/src/modules/updater/useUpdater.ts#L116). Test: work created during download/privilege wait prevents a transaction; cancellation/handoff never claims installation or relaunches.

9. **Definition of hidden web preview (stories 75-79).** Active-tab state does not cover a minimized window, while the existing inactive-window signal also includes mere loss of focus. Proposed: unload for inactive tab/Space or supported window-hidden/minimized signals; do not unload solely because a still-visible window loses focus. Do not promise complete Linux occlusion detection. Blocks hidden-preview acceptance, not viewer selection. Evidence: [preview visibility](https://github.com/kevsmir02/terax-ai/blob/cb48a3449a566d66725e2daecc4a21276e75b0ff/src/modules/preview/PreviewStack.tsx#L68), [frame lifecycle](https://github.com/kevsmir02/terax-ai/blob/cb48a3449a566d66725e2daecc4a21276e75b0ff/src/modules/preview/PreviewPane.tsx#L51). Test: tab/Space switch, minimize/restore and visible-window blur have distinct agreed outcomes; dev server survives.
